## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Enemy and encounter records
The system SHALL define `enemy` (name, hp, hp-max, armor, str, dex, wil, attack-die) and `encounter` (enemy, first-round, log, state) records.

#### Scenario: Create an enemy
- **WHEN** an enemy is created from bestiary data
- **THEN** it SHALL have hp, hp-max, armor, str, dex, wil, and attack-die fields

#### Scenario: Encounter tracks combat state
- **WHEN** an encounter is created
- **THEN** its state SHALL be `'active` and first-round SHALL be true

### Requirement: Attack resolution follows Cairn rules
The system SHALL resolve attacks by rolling the attacker's damage die, subtracting target armor, applying remainder to HP, then spillover to STR. At 0 STR the target is dead. Any STR damage triggers a critical save (d20 <= STR).

#### Scenario: Damage absorbed by armor
- **WHEN** a roll of 2 is made against a target with armor 3
- **THEN** 0 damage SHALL be dealt

#### Scenario: HP damage with no spillover
- **WHEN** a roll of 5 is made against a target with armor 1 and HP 6
- **THEN** 4 HP damage SHALL be dealt and no STR damage

#### Scenario: Spillover to STR
- **WHEN** a roll of 8 is made against a target with armor 1 and HP 3
- **THEN** 3 HP damage and 4 STR damage SHALL be dealt and a critical save SHALL be triggered

#### Scenario: Death at 0 STR
- **WHEN** STR damage reduces STR to 0
- **THEN** the target SHALL be marked as dead

### Requirement: Ability saves
The system SHALL provide str-save, dex-save, and wil-save functions that roll d20 <= the stat value to pass.

#### Scenario: Successful save
- **WHEN** a d20 roll is less than or equal to the stat
- **THEN** the save SHALL return true

### Requirement: Healing restores HP to max
The system SHALL provide a resolve-heal function that restores the player's HP to hp-max.

#### Scenario: Heal from partial HP
- **WHEN** player has 2/6 HP and heals
- **THEN** HP SHALL become 6/6 and healed amount SHALL be 4

### Requirement: Flee attempts use DEX save
The system SHALL provide a resolve-flee function. On success the player escapes. On failure the enemy gets a parting attack.

#### Scenario: Successful flee
- **WHEN** the player passes a DEX save
- **THEN** the flee SHALL succeed with no enemy attack

#### Scenario: Failed flee
- **WHEN** the player fails a DEX save
- **THEN** the enemy SHALL get a parting attack before the encounter ends

### Requirement: Combat choices built from inventory
The system SHALL generate combat choices by scanning the player's inventory. Weapons produce attack choices, consumables produce use choices, and flee is always available.

#### Scenario: Player with sword and herbs
- **WHEN** combat choices are generated for a player with a Sword (d8) and Healing Herbs x3
- **THEN** choices SHALL include "Attack with Sword (d8)", "Use Healing Herbs x3", and "Flee"

#### Scenario: Unarmed fallback
- **WHEN** player has no weapons
- **THEN** choices SHALL include "Unarmed Attack (d4)"

### Requirement: Encounter state machine
The system SHALL track encounter state transitions: active → victory (enemy STR=0 or failed save), death (player STR=0), incapacitated (player failed STR save), or fled.

#### Scenario: Victory on enemy death
- **WHEN** an attack reduces enemy STR to 0
- **THEN** encounter state SHALL become `'victory`

#### Scenario: Player death
- **WHEN** enemy attack reduces player STR to 0
- **THEN** encounter state SHALL become `'death`

### Requirement: Combat log formatting
The system SHALL format combat results as human-readable strings showing damage, critical hits, saves, and outcomes. String building SHALL use ECE string interpolation where it improves readability.

#### Scenario: Normal attack log
- **WHEN** player deals 4 HP damage and enemy deals 2 HP damage
- **THEN** the log SHALL include "You deal 4 damage." and "The enemy deals 2 damage."

#### Scenario: Combat stat display uses interpolation
- **WHEN** combat stats are displayed during an active encounter
- **THEN** enemy and player stats SHALL be rendered using string interpolation with record accessors, and function parameters SHALL NOT shadow record accessor names generated by `define-record`

#### Scenario: Enemy stats display works inside run-combat
- **WHEN** `run-combat` displays enemy stats using `$(enemy-name e)`
- **THEN** the expression SHALL call the `enemy-name` record accessor (not the function parameter) and display the enemy's name correctly
